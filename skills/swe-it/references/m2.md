# m2 Dispatch

Use m2 dispatch when the plan is explicitly m2-shaped,
budgeted, multi-week, manager-of-managers, or needs durable
resume behavior.

The prompt emitted by `swe-it` must include:

- `m2(<plan_source>)`.
- The plan source and repo root.
- A statement that this is execution, not planning or
  summary.
- The m2 durable conductor loop rule: continue until
  terminal state, explicit human stop, true blocker, or
  final-response gate permission.
- Acceptance-first planning before dispatch.
- Fanout through m2 managers and lower-level `swe-day` or
  `multi-swe-day` lanes when safe disjoint work exists.
- Ambiguity policy and review closeout.
- Final clean-HEAD verification before final response.

Do not treat PR creation, green validation, queued CI, or a
ready-for-review state as a stop condition for m2 dispatch.
Those are delivery events inside the conductor loop.

The durable conductor owns final verification after review
and address-comments changes land. It must validate
committed `HEAD` from a temporary detached clean worktree and
route missing-file or generated-artifact failures back into
implementation rather than final-answering from dirty
checkout evidence.
